# Chess Intuition Trainer: Extraction Edge Cases
# This file provides few-shot examples for Gemini 2.0 Flash to improve FEN and solution extraction accuracy.

---

### Case 1: Edge of Board Blindness (Top/Right Borders)
**Problem:** Pieces placed on the very edge of the diagram (Rank 8 or File H) are often omitted.
**Example (Puzzle 7):**
- **Image Context:** Black Rook on h8.
- **Initial Failure:** 8/p1r1k2r/1p5q/4Pp2/1P1Q2p1/6P1/3R1PK1/5R2 w - - 0 1 (Missed h8)
- **Correction:** 7r/p1r1k3/1p5q/4Pp2/1P1Q2p1/6P1/3R1PK1/5R2 w - - 0 1
- **Rationale:** Always scan the extreme boundaries of the 8x8 grid. If a solution mentions a square, verify the piece exists in the FEN.

---

### Case 2: Piece Misidentification (Queen vs. Bishop)
**Problem:** In low-resolution PDF scans, Bishops and Queens can look similar.
**Example (Puzzle 17):**
- **Image Context:** Black Bishop on f6.
- **Initial Failure:** r3k2r/p1p3p1/n3Nq1p/4pP2/8/2P3P1/PP2B1PP/4K2R w Kkq - 0 1 (Confused f6 Bishop for Queen; missed a1 Rook)
- **Correction:** r3k2r/ppp3p1/n3Nb1p/4pP2/8/2P3P1/PP2B1PP/R3K2R w KQkq - 0 1
- **Rationale:** Cross-reference piece types with the solution text. "Bxa6" implies a Bishop was present or reachable.

---

### Case 3: Vertical Coordinate Hallucination (Rank Shift)
**Problem:** Pieces are sometimes placed one rank too high or too low.
**Example (Puzzle 12):**
- **Image Context:** Black King on g7.
- **Initial Failure:** r5k1/ppq2p1p/2n3p1/8/3N3r/8/PP1Q1PPP/5RK1 w - - 0 1 (Placed King on g8 instead of g7)
- **Correction:** r7/ppq2pkp/2n3p1/8/3N3r/8/PP1Q1PPP/R4RK1 w - - 0 1
- **Rationale:** Verify the number of empty squares between pieces. A "back-rank mate" threat requires specific king placement.

---

### Case 4: Missing Interior Pawns
**Problem:** Small pawns in the middle of a crowded board are overlooked.
**Example (Puzzle 4):**
- **Image Context:** White Pawn on f3.
- **Initial Failure:** 1k6/ppq4p/5r2/4Q3/8/4N1P1/P5PP/7K w - - 0 1 (Missed f3 pawn)
- **Correction:** 1k6/ppq4p/5r2/4Q3/8/4NP2/P5PP/7K w - - 0 1
- **Rationale:** Detailed scanning of the center squares is required. Pawns often provide the "anchor" for tactical combinations.

---

### Case 5: Disconnect Between Solution and Board State
**Problem:** Extracting a FEN that makes the solution move illegal.
**Example (Puzzle 9):**
- **Image Context:** Black Rook on f6.
- **Initial Failure:** r6k/pp3Qpp/2bqp3/4Np2/2P5/8/PP3PPP/5RK1 w - - 0 1 (Missed f6)
- **Solution:** 1. Qxf6! gxf6 2. Nf7+
- **Correction:** r6k/pp3Qpp/2bqpr2/4Np2/2P5/8/PP3PPP/R4RK1 w - - 0 1
- **Rationale:** If the solution says "Qxf6", there MUST be a piece on f6 that the Queen can capture. Self-correct the FEN based on the solution move.
